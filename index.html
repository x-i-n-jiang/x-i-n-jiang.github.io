Xin Jiang

I use experiments, causal inference, and behavioral insights to study how people form beliefs, make decisions, and interact with AI.

    Strategic communication, belief formation, and decision-making under risk and uncertainty.
  • Human–AI Interaction
    How AI-generated information and assistance affect human reasoning, student learning, and social and economic decision-making.
